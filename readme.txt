# 👋 Hi, I'm Waris Oloyede Temitope!

I'm a **Software Engineer** passionate about building scalable, efficient, and user-centric applications that solve real-world problems. I specialize in **React, React Native, Next.js, and Node.js**, with a strong focus on frontend architecture, mobile development, performance optimization, and creating seamless user experiences.

- 🔭 **What I'm Working On:** Currently co-founding and building **Sphere NG**, a logistics platform connecting people who need to move goods with drivers through a location- and budget-based matching system. I'm also working as a **Mobile Engineer at EstateIQ**, developing solutions across estate management, access control, security, payments, and community management. Additionally, I contribute to real-estate technology products at **Myxellia**, building and maintaining applications such as Mainstone, Adozillion, and Doorhive.

- 💼 **Professional Experience:** Over 5 years of experience in software development, particularly within the React ecosystem. I've worked across multiple industries, translating complex business requirements into reliable, maintainable, and scalable software solutions.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Sc. in Computer Science with Mathematics from **Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), Nigeria**.

- 👯 **Collaboration Interests:** I'm always open to collaborating on impactful projects involving **React, React Native, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, and backend engineering**. I particularly enjoy working on products that leverage technology to solve practical problems and improve everyday experiences.

- 🌱 **What I'm Learning:** Continuously exploring better software architecture, system design, backend engineering, application security, and emerging technologies to become a more well-rounded engineer.

- ⚡ **Fun Fact:** I enjoy exploring new technologies, solving challenging engineering problems, and turning ideas into functional products. I believe great software is not just about writing code; it's about building solutions that make a difference.
